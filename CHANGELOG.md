# Changelog

All notable changes to the Cityzens App project are documented in this file.

## [1.0.1+2] - 2026-11-18

### Added
- **Academic Timetable & Conflict Resolver (Sprint 08)**:
  - SQLite persistence layer for class schedules with `timetable_slots` table.
  - Pairwise schedule interval overlap detector computing exact conflict durations.
  - Smart heuristic non-conflicting section recommendation algorithm.
  - One-tap section swapping to eliminate overlapping lecture hours.
  - Course enrollment toggle with real-time reactive schedule recalculation.
  - Interactive weekday selector (Mon-Fri) and search filter.
  - 360dp landscape master-detail architecture with independent scroll controllers.
  - Section detail inspection modal with room, instructor, and course metadata.
  - Automated unit and widget test suite covering all business logic and viewport safety.

## [1.0.0+1] - 2026-11-18

### Added
- Core Smart Campus Architecture:
  - Automated Attendance Ledger with QR scanning and cryptographically signed check-in.
  - Digital Library Records & Native Camera ISBN barcode scanner.
  - Smart Dining & Meal Plan Pre-ordering system.
  - AI Academic Companion & Syllabus Tutor with streaming response support.
  - Campus Shuttle & Transit Radar with live ETA tracking.
  - Smart Study Pod & Collaborative Room Booking.
  - Real-Time Campus Parking & EV Charging Radar.
  - Secure Offline-First Digital Student ID Card.
  - Exam Hall Pass with cryptographic HMAC validation and animated desk locator.
